Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a reply?

Standard grievances are addressed within 7-10 working days. RTI requests are processed within 30 days as per government norms.

Can I file an anonymous complaint?

For serious issues, you can choose to hide your identity from the public portal, but valid EPIC is required for verification.

What documents are needed for RTI?

A clear description of the information requested and your digital signature/voter ID proof is sufficient.

How can I track my request?

After submission, you will receive a Reference ID. Use this ID on the "Track Status" page to monitor progress.
